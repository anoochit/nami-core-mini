# DOOGEE Note 60W Space Edition

## Overview

The DOOGEE Note 60W (Widescreen series) is a budget-to-mid-range smartphone announced by DOOGEE in October 2026. The device features a wide aspect ratio display design inspired by widescreen slab phones such as the Huawei Pura X View. It made its public debut at major international trade exhibitions, including the 140th Canton Fair in Guangzhou, China, and the Thailand Mobile Expo 2026.

## Color Variants

The DOOGEE Note 60W comes in three colorways:
- **Space Grey** (commonly referred to as the Space edition)
- **Neptune Blue**
- **Moonlight Silver**

## Key Specifications

### Display & Design
- **Screen Size**: 6.38-inch LCD display
- **Refresh Rate**: 90Hz
- **Aspect Ratio**: Approximately 5:3 widescreen aspect ratio, providing wider horizontal viewing area for reading, browsing, and media consumption compared to traditional 19.5:9 or 20:9 aspect ratio phones
- **Thickness**: 8.4 mm
- **Rear Design**: Horizontal racetrack-shaped camera module with triple lenses and LED flash, featuring a textured rear back panel

### Battery & Power
- **Capacity**: 6,500 mAh battery
- **Charging**: USB Type-C fast charging

### Operating System & Software
- **OS**: Ships with Android 17 out of the box

### Memory & Storage
- **RAM**: Up to 12GB RAM (including extended virtual RAM support)
- **Internal Storage**: 256GB / 512GB UFS storage options

## Market Positioning

The DOOGEE Note 60W Space edition offers a wide-screen slab form factor with high battery capacity and modern Android software at an accessible entry-level to mid-range price point.
