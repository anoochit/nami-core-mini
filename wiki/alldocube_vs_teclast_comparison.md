# Alldocube iPlay 80 Mini vs Teclast P60 / T60 Mini Comparison

## Overview

This document compares the compact Android tablet offerings from Alldocube (specifically the iPlay 80 mini Turbo and iPlay 80 mini Pro) and Teclast (specifically the T60 Mini and P-series mini tablets).

## Key Specifications Comparison

### Display

- **Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo**: 8.8-inch IPS LCD, 2560 x 1600 (2.5K resolution), 120Hz refresh rate, 343 PPI, 400 nits brightness.
- **Teclast T60 Mini**: 8.8-inch IPS LCD, 2560 x 1600 (2.5K resolution), 90Hz refresh rate.
- **Teclast P50 Mini**: 8.7-inch IPS LCD, HD+ resolution, 90Hz refresh rate.

### Performance & Processor

- **Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo**: MediaTek Dimensity 7400 (4nm Octa-Core). Ideal for high-end 3D gaming and heavy multitasking.
- **Teclast T60 Mini**: UNISOC T7300 Octa-Core. Suitable for daily application efficiency, media playback, and light-to-moderate gaming.

### Memory & Storage

- **Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo**: 8GB physical RAM (expandable up to 16GB total via virtual memory), 128GB or 256GB internal storage, microSD card slot up to 2TB.
- **Teclast T60 Mini**: 8GB physical RAM (expandable up to 48GB total via virtual memory), 128GB internal storage, microSD card slot up to 2TB.

### Battery & Charging

- **Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo**: 6500mAh - 9020mAh battery (depending on regional edition) with USB Type-C fast charging.
- **Teclast T60 Mini**: 6000mAh battery with 18W PD fast charging over USB Type-C.

### Operating System & Connectivity

- **Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo**: Android 16 / 15, 4G LTE Dual SIM, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, GPS/BDS/GLONASS.
- **Teclast T60 Mini**: TeclastOS (Android 17 / 15 base), 4G LTE Dual SIM, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, GPS/BDS/GLONASS.

## Summary & Recommendation

- **Choose Alldocube iPlay 80 mini Turbo** for superior performance, higher display refresh rate (120Hz), and heavy gaming capability powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7400.
- **Choose Teclast T60 Mini / P-Series** for budget-conscious portable media consumption, e-reading, and light daily tasks.
